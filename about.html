More than a club.
A calling.

Whitehead Vultures Academy is a Christian-based academy with 30 active members aged 6-20 from Harare, Chitungwiza, Ruwa, Marondera and Norton. Moving youth from streets to pitch since 1996.

Our Story

What started as small community outreach in Mabelreign has grown into a beacon of hope. For 29 years, we've believed sport is not just a game — it's transformation.

Our goal is simple: provide healthy, disciplined, faith-filled alternative to drugs and alcohol while raising awareness about disease burden through sport. Every Tue, Thu & Sat at Mabelreign Girls High.

Mission

To create a faith-based academy to promote, inform and educate youth of Mabelreign and Harare. Moving youth from streets to pitch with excellence and evangelism.

Vision

To create a better healthier lifestyle for youth through excellence in sport that cuts across national, regional, cultural and socio-economic boundaries.

Core Values

Our foundation for 29 years:

  • • Evangelism & Faith
  • • Innovation & Creativity
  • • Diversity & Inclusivity
  • • Sportsmanship
  • • Organizational Governance

Faith • Excellence • Community

Every training starts with prayer, every game ends with thanksgiving. Sport is ministry teaching discipline, respect, and purpose for God's glory. From Mabelreign to Norton, raising a generation that chooses pitch over streets.
