Established in 1996 • Mabelreign, Harare

More than a club.
A calling.

Christian-based academy with 30 active members aged 6-20 years. We draw talent from Harare, Chitungwiza, Ruwa, Marondera and Norton to move youth from streets to pitch.

Our Story

What started in 1996 as small community outreach in Mabelreign has grown into beacon of hope. For 29 years, we've believed sport is not just a game — it's vehicle for transformation.

Our goal: provide healthy, disciplined, faith-filled alternative to drugs and alcohol while raising awareness about disease burden through sport. Every Tue, Thu & Sat at Mabelreign Girls High School.

Mission

To create a sports academy that is faith based to promote, inform and educate the youth of Mabelreign and Harare as a whole. Moving youth from streets to pitch with excellence.

👁️

Vision

To create a better healthier lifestyle for youth through excellence in sport that cuts across national, regional, cultural and social-economic boundaries. One child at a time.

More than athletes. Disciples. Every training starts with prayer, every game ends with thanksgiving. Sport is ministry teaching discipline, respect, purpose for God's glory. From Mabelreign to Norton, raising generation that chooses pitch over streets.
